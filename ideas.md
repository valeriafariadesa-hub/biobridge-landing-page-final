**Design Movement**: Neotropic Brutalism

**Core Principles**:
1.  **Raw Materiality**: Exposed, unpolished textures that evoke the Amazon's organic nature (wood grain, leaf veins) juxtaposed with stark, industrial typography.
2.  **High-Contrast Color Blocking**: A palette of deep emerald greens and burnt terracotta, used in large, flat blocks to create visual weight and urgency.
3.  **Asymmetric Grid**: A rigid, Swiss-style grid that is intentionally broken by organic, flowing shapes representing the Amazon river or forest canopy.
4.  **Functional Transparency**: Clear, direct messaging with no marketing fluff. The design should feel like a technical document, not a sales pitch.

**Color Philosophy**: The palette is derived from the Amazon itself. Deep Emerald (#00382B) represents the dense canopy and the seriousness of the mission. Vibrant Terracotta (#FF5E35) represents the urgency of the problem and the call to action. A soft off-white (#F1F7F5) provides a clean, breathable background.

**Layout Paradigm**: A single-page, vertically-scrolling experience with distinct, full-bleed sections. Each section is a "card" that presents one key message. The layout is asymmetric, with text often pushed to one side while a large, impactful image or graphic occupies the other.

**Signature Elements**:
1.  **Satellite Map Overlays**: Using actual satellite imagery of the Amazon as a recurring visual motif.
2.  **QR Code as a Design Element**: The "Compliance Passport" QR code is stylized and used as a decorative element, symbolizing the product's output.

**Interaction Philosophy**: Minimal, purposeful interactions. Scroll-triggered reveals for key statistics. No gratuitous animations.

**Animation**: Subtle fade-in and slide-up animations for text blocks as they enter the viewport. A subtle parallax effect on background images.

**Typography System**: `Montserrat` for headlines (Bold 900, 64px-48px) for its strong, geometric presence. `Inter` for body text (Regular 400, 18px-16px) for maximum readability.

**Signature Elements**:
1.  **Animated GPS Path**: A line that traces a path across a map, showing the journey of a product from the forest to the European market.

**Design Movement**: Stark Regulatory Tech

**Core Principles**:
1.  **Institutional Credibility**: A design language that evokes official EU documents and regulatory frameworks, building trust with German importers.
2.  **Monochromatic Foundation**: A stark black, white, and grey palette that signals seriousness and professionalism.
3.  **Sharp Geometry**: Clean lines, 90-degree angles, and a strict grid. No rounded corners.
4.  **Accent as Signal**: A single, vibrant accent color (e.g., EU Blue or a warning orange) used sparingly to highlight calls to action and critical information.

**Color Philosophy**: A foundation of pure white (#FFFFFF) and deep black (#000000) for maximum contrast and readability. A single accent of "EU Blue" (#003399) or "Alert Orange" (#FF6600) is used only for interactive elements and key data points.

**Layout Paradigm**: A document-like structure with clear headers, subheaders, and body text. Content is organized into distinct, bordered sections, similar to a legal document or a technical specification sheet.

**Signature Elements**:
1.  **Official Seal/Badge**: A stylized badge or seal that represents the "Compliance Passport," giving it an air of official certification.
2.  **Regulatory Citation Blocks**: Styled blockquotes that cite specific EUDR articles, lending credibility and authority.

**Interaction Philosophy**: Functional and efficient. No unnecessary animations. Interactions are limited to clear button clicks and form submissions.

**Animation**: None. The page is static, reinforcing the serious, official tone.

**Typography System**: `Roboto` for all text (Bold 700 for headlines, Regular 400 for body) for its neutral, institutional feel. Alternatively, `DM Sans` for a slightly more modern take.

## Selected Design: Neotropic Brutalism

I will proceed with the **Neotropic Brutalism** design philosophy. This approach best captures the urgency of the EUDR problem while grounding the solution in the tangible reality of the Amazon. The high-contrast color blocking and asymmetric layouts will create a visually striking and memorable landing page that differentiates the startup from generic SaaS competitors.
